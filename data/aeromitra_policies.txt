AEROMITRA SOCIAL MEDIA RESPONSE POLICIES (SYNTHETIC, FOR EDUCATIONAL PROTOTYPE ONLY)
AeroMitra is a fictional airline. These policies are made up for a class project and are not legal advice.

[POLICY 1: BRAND VOICE]
Reply in a warm, calm and respectful tone. Use the passenger's language: English, Hindi or simple Hinglish. Keep replies under 60 words. Say sorry once, then move to the next step. Never argue, joke, blame the passenger or blame staff in public. Never share booking details in a public reply; ask the passenger to send a direct message.

[POLICY 2: DELAYS AND CANCELLATIONS]
For delays above 2 hours, AeroMitra shares updates on the app and by SMS and offers a meal voucher. For delays above 4 hours, the passenger can ask for a free rebooking. If AeroMitra cancels a flight, the passenger can choose a free rebooking on the next available flight or a full refund. Public replies must not promise cash compensation. Ask the passenger to direct message the booking ID so the care team can check the case.

[POLICY 3: REFUNDS]
Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method within 7 working days. Refund status can be checked on the AeroMitra app under My Trips. If a refund takes longer than 7 working days, the case is escalated to the Refund Desk within 24 hours. Staff must not quote a refund amount in a public reply.

[POLICY 4: BAGGAGE]
Delayed baggage must be reported at the airport baggage desk or on the app within 24 hours of landing. AeroMitra tracks the bag and delivers it to the passenger's address at no cost once found. Damage claims need photos and the baggage tag. Public replies must not promise a claim amount.

[POLICY 5: SAFETY, MEDICAL, DISCRIMINATION AND LEGAL THREATS]
Any post about food safety, injury, medical emergencies, aircraft safety, discrimination, harassment or a legal or media threat is Priority 1 (P1). P1 posts go to the Duty Manager and the Legal and Safety team within 30 minutes. No automatic reply is sent. A human drafts a short public holding reply: acknowledge, apologise, say the matter is being reviewed, and ask for a direct message. Never admit fault or liability and never speculate about the cause in public.

[POLICY 6: PRAISE AND GENERAL QUERIES]
Thank the passenger and, if they mention a crew member or team, say the feedback will be passed on. For general queries on baggage limits, web check-in (opens 48 hours before departure) or date changes, point to the AeroMitra app or website. If the answer is not in these policies, say the care team will follow up by direct message. Do not guess.
